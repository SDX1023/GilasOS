# Tips and Tricks

Here are some advanced features to help you get the most out of StudyOS.

## Spaced Repetition

The flashcard system uses the SM-2 algorithm for spaced repetition:

- **Again** (quality 1): Reset to beginning
- **Hard** (quality 3): Increase interval slightly
- **Good** (quality 4): Normal progression
- **Easy** (quality 5): Skip ahead in intervals

Your progress is saved in localStorage, so you can pick up where you left off!

## Pomodoro Timer

Use the built-in Pomodoro timer to stay focused:

1. Click **Start** to begin a 25-minute work session
2. Take a 5-minute short break
3. After 4 sessions, take a 15-minute long break
4. Customize durations in the settings

## PDF to Flashcards

Generate flashcards from your study materials:

1. Go to **Tools > PDF Cards**
2. Paste your API key (OpenAI or Anthropic)
3. Paste or upload your content
4. Edit and save generated cards

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- `Ctrl + K` - Open search
- `Space` - Flip flashcard
- `1-4` - Rate card difficulty
- `←` `→` - Navigate between cards

## Dark Mode

Toggle dark mode using the sun/moon icon in the navigation bar.

Your preference is saved automatically!
